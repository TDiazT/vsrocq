# Get help

When something does not work, collect:

- the setup report: "Copy the setup report" on the left, then "Copy to clipboard",
- the log: "Show log" on the left,
- the smallest `.v` file that shows the problem.

Then ask on the [Rocq Zulip](https://rocq-prover.zulipchat.com/), in the "VsRocq devs & users" channel, or open an issue at [github.com/rocq-prover/vsrocq/issues](https://github.com/rocq-prover/vsrocq/issues).

You can reopen this guide at any time with "Rocq: Docs: Show setup guide" in the Command Palette.
